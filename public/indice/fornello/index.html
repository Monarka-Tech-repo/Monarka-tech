The Restaurant

A Saint-Aurèle Classic Since 1974!

It started with one wood oven and twelve tables on Rue du Marché. Fifty years on, the Ferraro family still lights that oven every morning, below three dining rooms, a busy bar and a rooftop terrace overlooking the river.
