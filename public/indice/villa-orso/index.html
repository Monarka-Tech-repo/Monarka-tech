IThe house

Built for a coffee merchant in 1904.

Four floors around a courtyard, terrazzo stairs worn smooth and a façade the colour of old cream. We spent three years bringing it back, one door handle at a time.

IIThe rooms

Eight apartments, no two alike.

Each one is named after the room that defines it and furnished with pieces that already had a life before you arrived.

01/ 03

Ciao, I'm Lena

I grew up two streets from here, in a house that always had one more chair at the table. Villa Orso is my attempt to give you that chair.

Every apartment is furnished with pieces I found at flea markets along the Karst, restored by hand and lit the way evenings should be lit. I live on the top floor, so if you need a recommendation, an umbrella or a corkscrew, just knock.

What I care about

3Principles
1

Light

Lamps at eye level, never a ceiling spotlight in sight. Linen curtains that let the morning in slowly, and dimmers in every room.

2

Patina

Original terrazzo, scuffed parquet and doors with a hundred years of fingerprints. I restore, I don't replace.

3

Quiet

Thick walls, courtyard bedrooms and no televisions on the wall. The loudest thing here is the espresso pot.

Location

All eight apartments sit in one restored 1904 building in Cittavecchia, a few steps from Piazza Unità, the seafront and the best morning market in town.
