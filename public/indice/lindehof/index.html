Where work meets comfort.

We turned an old dairy farm into a calm place for people who work hard and travel often.

The stables are now meeting rooms with long oak tables. The hayloft holds eight bedrooms under the original beams. Outside, there are orchards, a vegetable garden and a few animals who like visitors.

You get the focus of a retreat and the practical details of a business hotel: fast Wi-Fi, good coffee and a team that handles the rest.
