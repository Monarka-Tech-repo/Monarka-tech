01 · Groundwork

Every skyline starts in the dirt.

Before any steel goes up, we test the soil, plan the logistics and pour a foundation built to carry the full weight of the tower, with a wide safety margin.

Preconstruction is where we save clients the most money. Phasing, crane placement and delivery routes are planned months before the first truck arrives.

02 · Structure

One floor a week. Lifted, set, bolted.

Our tower cranes climb with the building. They lift each steel beam from the laydown yard and set it in place, so the frame rises at a steady pace.
