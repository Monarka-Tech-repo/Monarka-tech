      Elio Varenne, freelance interactive developer. Movement is the message.

      (About)

      With a designer’s eye for rhythm and timing, I build websites where every transition has a reason to exist. I spend the time it takes to get the details right: the weight of a scroll, the delay of a hover, the first second of a page.

      I’m the founder of OSTRA, a small journal about slow living and hand-made objects. Before going independent I worked at Northlight Studio and Pixel & Grain, and I’ve been a web awards jury member since 2021.

      Open to collaborations with studios, agencies and brands from autumn 2026.
