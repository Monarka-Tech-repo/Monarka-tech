About Meridian

Built for clients with national needs.

Meridian is a group of seven separately owned construction firms. Each one is a leading builder in its own region, and together we serve clients who build in many markets.

Our partners share best practices, safety programs and pricing data. They also share relationships with local trades. Clients get one point of contact and consistent standards everywhere, with a team on site that knows the local codes, labor market and suppliers.
